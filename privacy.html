Soothing Bots Privacy Policy

Effective date: 20 November 2026
Developer and publisher: Clarke Plumo
("we", "us")
Contact: clarke.plumo@gmail.com

Soothing Bots is a mixed-reality music game for Meta Quest: a choir of friendly robot heads sings songs you wake, mute and arrange with your hands. It is made for players aged 10 and up: teens and adults, and children aged 10 to 12 (section 6). This policy explains exactly what data the game collects and processes, what it is used for, where it is kept, for how long, and how you can ask for it to be deleted.

In short. The game collects and processes some data about you: through Meta's platform it checks that your copy is genuine, sells and grants diamonds (in-app purchases) and reads your Meta account's age category; on your headset it saves your songs, settings, diamonds and a record of your purchases; and it uses your hands (and, if you allow it, your eyes and the passthrough camera) live while you play. We have no servers and no accounts of our own: the game does not send any of this data to us, and we do not sell or share it with anyone. It has no ads and no analytics of ours.

1. Data the app collects and processes

For each kind of data: what it is, where it comes from, what it is used for, where it is kept, whether it is shared, and how long it is kept.

1.1 Purchase data: in-app purchases of diamonds

  • What: the diamond packs on sale and their prices in your currency; the diamond purchases made with your Meta account in this game (for each one: Meta's purchase ID, which pack it was, when it was granted, and whether it has been used up). Payment details (card, billing address) are handled entirely by Meta and are never seen by the game.
  • Source: Meta's Platform SDK (In-App Purchases), from your Meta account.
  • Purpose: to show the shop, start Meta's checkout when you buy a pack, and add the diamonds you bought to your balance exactly once (so a purchase is never lost or granted twice).
  • Where it is kept: the game keeps a record of the purchases it has granted, and your diamond balance, in its own storage on your headset. Meta keeps the purchase itself with your Meta account.
  • Shared: no. The game does not send it to us or anyone else. Meta processes the purchase under its own privacy policy (section 4).
  • How long: the record on your headset is kept until you uninstall the game or clear its data. Meta keeps its records under its own policy.

1.2 Ownership check (entitlement)

  • What: a yes/no answer from Meta: does the Meta account on this headset own the game?
  • Source: Meta's Platform SDK (Entitlements).
  • Purpose: to check at launch that your copy is genuine. It is never used to offer you anything.
  • Where it is kept: in the game's memory only, while it runs. It is never written to the headset.
  • Shared: no.
  • How long: forgotten when the game closes.

1.3 Your Meta account's age category

  • What: Meta's age category for the Meta account in use: child aged 10 to 12, teen, adult, or unknown.
  • Source: Meta's Platform SDK (User Age Category).
  • Purpose: only to hide the diamond shop and every way to buy diamonds from children aged 10 to 12 (section 6). It is used for nothing else.
  • Where it is kept: in the game's memory only, while it runs. It is never written to the headset.
  • Shared: no.
  • How long: forgotten when the game closes; the game asks Meta again the next time it starts.

1.4 Your Meta account identifier

  • What: the app-scoped user ID that Meta's platform uses to identify your Meta account to this game.
  • Source: Meta's Platform SDK.
  • Purpose: Meta's platform uses it to answer the game's requests above (your purchases, ownership and age category belong to your account). The game's own code does not read, display or save your user ID, and does not use it to identify or track you.
  • Where it is kept: held by Meta. The game does not store it.
  • Shared: no.
  • How long: the game keeps nothing; Meta keeps it under its own policy.

1.5 Your songs, settings and game progress

  • What: your songs (projects and your edits), the robots' places in your room (the headset's spatial anchors and their positions), your settings and tips already seen, your diamond balance and the daily free-diamond timer.
  • Source: you and the game, on the headset.
  • Purpose: to keep your songs, robots and diamonds the way you left them.
  • Where it is kept: on your headset only. We never receive it.
  • Shared: no. (You can choose to share a song yourself as a QR code or text code; it contains only the song's settings and your edits, never information about you.)
  • How long: until you uninstall the game or clear its data.

1.6 Hand tracking

  • What: your hand and finger positions from the headset's hand tracking.
  • Source: the headset.
  • Purpose: pinching, poking buttons, carrying robots, hand signs (such as a wave or thumbs up) and hand games with the robots.
  • Where it is kept: processed frame by frame on your headset, in memory only. Never stored.
  • Shared: no. Never sent anywhere.
  • How long: discarded immediately.

1.7 Eye tracking (only on headsets that have it, and only if you allow it)

  • What: where you are looking.
  • Source: the headset's eye tracking (for example Meta Quest Pro), only if you allow it when the system asks. Meta Quest 2, 3 and 3S have no eye tracking.
  • Purpose: only to pick the robot or button you pinch. Without eye tracking, or if you decline, the game uses your head direction instead.
  • Where it is kept: processed frame by frame on your headset, in memory only. Never stored.
  • Shared: no. Never sent anywhere, and never used for advertising, profiling or analytics.
  • How long: discarded immediately. You can turn eye tracking off at any time in your headset's settings.

1.8 Passthrough camera images (Import by QR only)

  • What: images from the headset's passthrough cameras (Meta Quest 3 and 3S).
  • Source: the headset, only with your permission: the game asks the first time you tap Import by QR.
  • Purpose: only to read a song's QR code while you scan. The camera stops as soon as you close the scanner.
  • Where it is kept: processed on your headset, in memory only. Never stored.
  • Shared: no. Never sent over the network and never used for anything else.
  • How long: each image is discarded immediately. You can type a song's text code instead, and you can withdraw the camera permission at any time in your headset's settings.

2. What the app does not collect or do

  • No account or sign-up with us. We do not ask for your name, email, date of birth, location, contacts or payment details.
  • No microphone use: the robots sing, you don't have to.
  • No profile, friends or other Meta account data: the game uses only the Meta platform features listed in section 1.
  • No advertising, no ad identifiers, no tracking across apps.
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reports of ours. (Meta and Unity may collect diagnostics of their own: section 3.)
  • No selling or sharing of personal information.

3. Diagnostics by Meta and Unity

The game is built with Meta's XR SDK (which includes Meta's own telemetry) and the Unity engine. The game itself sends nothing to us, but Meta's platform and SDK may collect diagnostics and usage information about the app and your headset, under Meta's privacy policy (section 4). Unity's engine diagnostics are switched on, so basic engine and device diagnostics may go to Unity under its privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/privacy-policy. We do not receive this data. The game uses no Unity Analytics, no Unity advertising and no other third-party analytics or advertising service.

4. Meta

Meta operates the Meta Horizon Store, the Meta platform services the game uses (section 1.1 to 1.4) and your headset. Meta handles your Meta account, payments, purchases, age information and device data under its own privacy policy: https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/.

5. How to request deletion of your data

Ask us. Email clarke.plumo@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion – Soothing Bots". We will answer within 30 days. We do not run servers or keep a copy of your data, so we will tell you exactly how to remove what exists and help with anything that is unclear. We do not need any personal details from you beyond your email, and we will not keep your request longer than needed to answer it.

What deleting means for this game:

  • Data on your headset (songs, settings, robot places, diamond balance, daily-diamond timer and the record of granted purchases, section 1.1 and 1.5): delete it yourself at any time by uninstalling the game, or by clearing the game's storage in your headset's app settings, where your headset offers it. This removes it completely; we hold no other copy. Note that your diamond balance, including diamonds you bought, is lost when this data is deleted.
  • Data used live (hand tracking, eye tracking, camera images, the ownership check and the age category, section 1.2, 1.3, 1.6 to 1.8): never stored, so there is nothing left to delete once the game closes.
  • Data held by Meta (your purchase and ownership records, your Meta account identifier and age information, section 1.1 to 1.4): these belong to your Meta account and are kept by Meta, not by us. You can see, download or delete your Meta account data, or delete your Meta account, with Meta's tools:
    • Meta Accounts Center: https://accountscenter.meta.com/ (Your information and permissions)
    • Deleting your Meta account or Meta Horizon profile: https://www.meta.com/help/quest/articles/accounts/account-settings-and-management/delete-meta-account/
    • Meta's privacy policy, including how to contact Meta about your data: https://www.meta.com/legal/privacy-policy/

6. Ages and children

Soothing Bots is for players aged 10 and up. On the Meta Horizon Store it is listed for Mixed Ages: teens and adults (13 and over) and children aged 10 to 12. It is not meant for children under 10, and Meta does not allow children under 10 to use Meta Quest.

Players aged 10 to 12 can't make purchases. Each time the game starts, it asks Meta for the age category of the Meta account in use (section 1.3). When Meta says the account belongs to a child aged 10 to 12, the diamond shop and every way to buy diamonds are hidden, and the game says when the next free diamond comes instead. The free daily diamond and everything else work as for everyone. When Meta gives no answer (for example offline), the game treats the player as 13 or older.

The game processes the same data for every player, as described in section 1, and sends none of it to us. Meta handles Meta accounts, their ages, purchases and parental supervision (for example Meta's Family Center) under its own privacy policy (section 4). A parent or guardian should set up and supervise the headset for players aged 10 to 12, and can request deletion as described in section 5.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the CCPA/CPRA), you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to object to its use. Because the game keeps your data on your headset and Meta keeps your account data, most of these rights are exercised on the headset (section 5) or with Meta (section 4). Contact us at clarke.plumo@gmail.com with any request or question and we will answer within 30 days.
