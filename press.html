About the game

Soothing Bots is a mixed-reality music experience for Meta Quest. A choir of kind little robot heads moves into your room and sings you story songs. Wake them, hush them and arrange them with your hands or your controllers.

Features

  • Sixteen robots, one neighbourhood. Every song is sung by the same cast: a family, their grandparents, aunties and neighbours. Meet Teddy, who knows every dinosaur; Grandma Pearl, who can't say no; Grandpa Lou, who runs the diner; and Murphy, the unluckiest delivery driver in town.
  • Twelve story songs. Tap Random for an episode such as Free Refills or Murphy's Lucky Day, and the robots act it out as they sing. Or type your own words and the choir sings them back.
  • Hands or controllers. Look at a robot and pinch, or pull the trigger, to wake it. Poke to hush it, or pick it up and carry it. With hand tracking, wave or make a heart and the robots answer.
  • Arrange the music. Choose who sings the tune, change the chords, key and tempo, and edit a robot's line.
  • Share a song with one QR code. A Quest 3 or 3S scans it with the headset camera; on any headset you can type the text code instead.
  • Free to download. A free diamond about once a day makes one new random song. Extra diamonds are optional in-app purchases.

No accounts, no ads, and your songs stay on your headset.

Notes for reviewers

  • Colour passthrough on Quest 3, 3S and Pro; Quest 2's passthrough is greyscale.
  • Scanning a QR code needs a Quest 3 or 3S. Every headset can type the text code.
  • Hand signs (wave, heart, thumbs up) need hand tracking.
  • Each new Random song uses one diamond; the free daily diamond is enough. Diamonds are never needed to play, share or open existing songs.
