Soothing Bots

A choir of kind little robot heads moves into your room and sings you story songs. Wake them, hush them and arrange them with your hands.

Coming soon toMeta Quest

For Meta Quest 3 and 3S. Mixed reality, hands only: no controllers needed.

Your own little choir

Gentle enough for small ears, silly enough for grown-ups, and every song is a bit different.

A robot choir in your room

Up to 16 robot heads, each with its own face, voice and part, sit right there in your real room. Some hum the low notes, some sing the tune, and one or two can't resist a "doo wop".

Put them on the bookshelf, the sofa or the windowsill. They stay where you left them.

Story songs for the whole family

Every song is sung by the same sixteen robots, a family and their neighbourhood. Meet Teddy, almost four, who knows every dinosaur but can't do up his shoes yet; his big sister Ivy; Grandma Pearl, who can't say no; and Grandpa Lou, who runs the diner and says "That's extra."

Tap Random for a little story episode, like Free Refills, Just a Box or Murphy's Lucky Day. Everyone gets a hook to sing and plenty of silly bits, and a quiet moment in the bridge. Or type in your own words and the choir will sing them back to you.

The app is free to download. You get a free diamond about once a day, and one diamond makes one new random song; if you want more, extra diamonds are an optional purchase in the app.

Just your hands

No controllers, no buttons to learn. Look at a robot and pinch to wake it. Poke to hush it. Pick one up and carry it somewhere new.

Wave hello or give a thumbs up and the robots answer you back.

Share songs as QR codes

Made something lovely? Turn it into one QR code and show it to a friend. Their Quest 3 or 3S scans it and their choir sings your song. (Only a very heavily edited song needs a few codes.)

The code is drawn inside your headset, so to show it to a friend, cast your view to a phone (with the Meta Horizon app) and hold the phone up to their headset.

The code holds the song and nothing else. There's a text code too, for when a camera isn't handy.

How it plays

Five minutes or an hour: it's up to you (and the robots).

  1. Meet the sleepy choir

    Put on your headset. A row of robots is dozing in your room, waiting for you.

  2. Look and pinch

    Look at a robot and pinch. It wakes with a yawn and joins the song.

  3. Arrange the music

    Poke to hush a voice, wake another, change who sings the tune, and move everyone around.

  4. Pass it on

    Happy with your song? Share it as a QR code so a friend's choir can sing it too.

No accounts, no ads and no tracking. Everything you make stays on your headset. Read the privacy policy.
